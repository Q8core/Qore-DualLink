# Qore DualLink — use ALL your internet connections at once

**Free · Windows · Linux · one file, no install needed**

<sub>Live view, with sample speeds shown</sub>

Plug in everything you have: Ethernet (built-in or through a USB adapter or hub), Wi-Fi (built-in or USB), and phones by USB or hotspot. Qore DualLink uses **up to 16 connections at the same time**. Each new connection goes out over the next of your lines, so downloads, updates, download managers and multi-connection speed tests get the **sum** of your lines.

**New in 1.0.16: Live view.** Each connection appears as a coloured line flowing into one combined total, with its real download and upload speed, how many lines are working, and a one-minute chart.

## Good to know

- It adds speed only with **different internet sources**, for example home internet plus a phone's 4G/5G. Two connections to the same router share one line.
- **Not faster:** a single stream, such as one video, uses one line at a time.
- **Gaming:** a game or call stays on one line. For the steadiest ping, play on one connection with Combine off.
- *Combine all traffic* asks for admin rights (Windows) or your password (Linux). Turning it off, or closing the app, puts everything back.
